### What does Lukol Search Engine do?

Lukol Search Engine is a simple web and image search engine that uses Google Custom Search to help users find publicly indexed material relevant to an investigation.&#x20;

It has historically been marketed as an anonymous search engine, including descriptions of proxy-based searching. However, its current privacy policy mentions cookies, advertising and analytics partners without providing enough technical detail to independently verify the extent of any search-query anonymisation. You should therefore not assume it guarantees anonymity.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a privacy-focused search engine but be mindful of potential cookies affecting complete anonymisation. &#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Lukol Search Engine can be useful for:

* Discovering publicly indexed webpages mentioning a person, organisation, event or subject.
* Finding relevant articles, reference material and other online sources for background research.
* Conducting image searches to identify potentially relevant photographs and illustrations.
* Locating pages containing distinctive phrases or alternative names associated with an investigation.
* Generating additional URLs and source leads for follow-up research.
* Comparing search results with those from Google, Bing or other search engines to identify potentially overlooked material.
* Supporting research documentation by recording the query used, date searched and original source URLs.

### Summary

Lukol Search Engine fits primarily into the discovery stage of the OSINT workflow with its main value in providing a straightforward way to search for public webpages and images. However, it should be considered a supplementary search interface rather than a specialist investigative platform or a guaranteed anonymous search service.

### Ethical Considerations

* Verify important statements against the original webpage or authoritative primary sources.
* Do not assume that Lukol guarantees anonymity or prevents search activity from being associated with a user.
* Avoid entering confidential investigative details, sensitive personal information or unique search terms where disclosure could compromise the investigation.
* Assess image provenance, publication context and rights before reproducing images in reports.
